This is a design prototype for the landing view of the new MONEY tab — not a replacement for the homepage, which stays exactly as it is. The figures are illustrative placeholders standing in for the daily ingest described in PLAN.md — in the built version every number carries a source and a vintage, and cross-layer attribution weights are published rather than assumed.
